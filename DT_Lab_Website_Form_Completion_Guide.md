# DT Lab Website Information Form

## A guide for lab members

Thank you for helping us keep the DT Lab website accurate and up to date.

The workbook already contains information currently shown or planned for the website. Please review the information relevant to you, correct anything that is inaccurate, and add any missing details. You do not need to complete every worksheet—only the sections that apply to you.

## Before you begin

- Open `DT_Lab_Website_Information_Collection_Form.xlsx`.
- Read the **Start Here** worksheet first.
- Only provide information that you are comfortable publishing on the public lab website.
- Complete the **Completed / confirmed by** field so we know whom to contact if clarification is needed.
- If you are unsure about a field, leave it blank and explain the issue briefly in **Notes**.

## Understanding the status options

Please select one status for every row you review:

- **Please review** — The information still needs to be checked.
- **Confirmed** — The current information is accurate and may be published.
- **Updated** — You have corrected or replaced some information in the row.
- **New entry** — This is new information that is not yet on the website.
- **Do not publish** — This information should not appear on the public website.
- **Not applicable** — The field or row does not apply.

## How to complete each worksheet

### 1. Lab Details

This worksheet is mainly for lab leaders or administrators.

Please review the lab name, short description, university and school affiliation, public contact details, postal address, and online profiles. Enter the final wording in **Your confirmed or updated content**, even if it is similar to the current wording.

### 2. Lab Members

Find your existing row or add a new row at the bottom.

Please check:

- Your full name and preferred title
- Your role in the lab
- Your affiliation
- Research interests
- Short biography
- Public email address
- Personal or university profile
- Google Scholar, GitHub, and LinkedIn links
- Location
- Profile photo file name

Required fields are marked in the column headings. Optional fields may be left blank.

For research interests, use a short list of topics or one concise sentence. For the biography, two or three sentences are usually enough. Please write in the third person and use clear, professional English.

If you provide a profile photo, use a clear JPG or PNG image. A square or portrait-oriented image is preferred. Enter the exact file name in the workbook and submit the image together with the form.

### 3. Research Themes

This worksheet is mainly for lab leaders and research theme coordinators.

Please confirm the theme title and provide a clear description of approximately 25–60 words. The wording should be understandable to researchers, students, industry partners, and other website visitors. Add a few representative keywords if helpful, and indicate whether the theme should appear on the home page.

### 4. Publications

Add one publication per row.

Please copy details from the official publication record whenever possible:

- Full paper title
- Authors in the published order
- Conference or journal name
- Publication year
- Publication type
- DOI, arXiv, PDF, code, or project links

A short summary or highlight is optional. It should explain the main contribution in one or two sentences without making unsupported claims.

### 5. News

Add one news item per row. Suitable items include:

- Paper acceptances or presentations
- Awards and honours
- Grants and funded projects
- New lab members
- Invited talks and events
- Dataset, software, or project releases
- Significant collaborations or achievements

Use the date format `YYYY-MM-DD`, for example `2026-07-31`. Keep the headline short and write a concise, factual description. Add a supporting link or image when available.

## Links, dates, and writing style

- Enter complete links beginning with `https://` or `http://`.
- Enter email addresses as `name@example.edu`.
- Use `YYYY-MM-DD` for dates.
- Use the official spelling of names, venues, organisations, and titles.
- Keep descriptions concise, factual, and suitable for a public academic website.
- Avoid confidential, unpublished, sensitive, or personally identifying information that should not be public.

## Before returning the form

Please check that:

- Your information is accurate and approved for public use.
- Required fields have been completed.
- Each reviewed row has an appropriate status.
- Your name appears in **Completed / confirmed by**.
- Photo and other supporting files are attached and match the file names entered.
- Links open correctly.
- Any uncertainty or special request is explained in **Notes**.

Thank you for contributing to the DT Lab website.
