# About

The Data Transpose (DT) Lab at Adelaide University conducts AI, big data, and cloud-computing research aimed at transforming industries through advanced technology. Lab members are affiliated with the Australian Institute for Machine Learning, and current work includes collaborations in health and medical data.

## Research directions

- Model calibration and uncertainty estimation.
- Fairness, adversarial robustness, privacy, interpretability, and explainability.
- Natural language processing, text mining, and causal reasoning.
- Multimodal generation, information retrieval, federated learning in NLP, and Artificial Intelligence of Things.
- Medical data, multimodal information fusion, and learning from imperfect data.
- Foundation model alignment, trustworthy machine learning, and machine unlearning.
- Efficient training and inference, including quantization, pruning, knowledge distillation, and neural architecture search.
- Hyperparameter optimization and advanced optimization algorithms.
- Transfer, continual, meta-, and few-shot learning.
- Hardware-aware AI, edge computing, TinyML, and reinforcement learning.
- Big data, distributed systems, and cloud computing.

## Collaboration

We welcome collaboration with researchers, students, industry, health organizations, and government partners whose work overlaps with our research areas.

The lab leaders are eligible to supervise Master's and PhD students. Prospective students can review their official Adelaide University profiles on the [People page]({{ '/people/' | relative_url }}) and contact them to discuss availability.
